# ALL FOR ONE: Marketing summary

Full plan (Doc): https://claude.ai/code/artifact/ee049430-ef8f-45f1-a655-ce2785e38f4a
Note: that plan was written before the price check, alerts and flip redesign. Lead with "Never overpay. Never miss a deal."

## Message
- "Never overpay. Never miss a deal."
- "Same item. Different price on every app."
- "Is this a rip-off? Check it in 5 seconds." (price check)
- For flippers: "See your profit before you buy."

## Channels
- **TikTok first.** Repost the same videos to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
- **Business account.** Pin 3 videos: what it is, a real price find, and how to use it in 3 taps.
- **Small UK thrift and sneaker creators** (5k–50k followers): offer free Pro or £20–£50 per video. Label these as ads.

## Content formats (10–20 seconds, captions on, hook in the first second)
1. Price check: is this a good deal?
2. Guess the cheapest app.
3. "Find it for me," replying to comments.
4. Don't get scammed: one tip per video.
5. Flip maths: bought for £X, sold for £Y, profit after fees.
6. Polished AI ads from Higgsfield. Label them as AI. Never show an AI person as a real customer.

## Paid ads
- **£0/month:** post 1–2 times a day.
- **£100/month:** boost the best 2 free videos.
- **£200/month:** test 3 ads at £5 a day, then put the money behind the winner.
- **Targeting:** UK, 18–34, interested in trainers, gaming, fashion and cars.
- **Stop rule:** turn an ad off if it spends £20 without a sign-up.
- **Keep the cost to win a paying subscriber under about £12.**

## Funnel
1. Video, then the link in bio.
2. Free account: 3 searches and 3 price checks a day.
3. They hit the limit or want more alerts, and the paywall appears.
4. 7-day trial with a card.
5. The trial converts to paid.

**Extras:** email a reminder 2 days before the trial ends, offer "give a month, get a month" referrals, and ask why when someone cancels.

## Rough numbers (estimates, not promises)
- Each subscriber brings in about £3.60 a month after Stripe fees, with a 50/50 monthly/yearly mix. That's before VAT and tax.
- **100 subscribers:** about £360 a month. **1,000:** about £3,600 a month.
- VAT registration is required above £90,000 a year.
- Typically 2–5% of free users pay, so 1,000 subscribers needs about 20,000–50,000 free users.

## Rules
- Don't show eBay, Vinted or Facebook logos in ads without permission.
- No fake savings. Only show prices you actually found.
- Check that the "ALL FOR ONE" trademark is clear before spending on branding.
